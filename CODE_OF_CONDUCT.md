# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in Ornyx Linux issues, reviews and other project spaces is expected to treat others with respect. Welcome questions, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and give feedback on the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Report incidents privately to a maintainer through a contact method they list on their GitHub profile. If the report involves that maintainer, contact another maintainer if available or use GitHub's Report abuse control. Include relevant links and share only the personal information needed to assess the report.

Maintainers will review reports discreetly, hear relevant context and take proportionate action. Actions may include requesting a correction, removing harmful content or restricting participation.
